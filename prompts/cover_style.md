Viral Persian news-channel YouTube/Instagram thumbnail. Bold, busy, dramatic collage composition.
The main subject (the person or object of the story) is BIG in the upper half: close-up, sharp, slightly angled,
with strong cinematic rim light; it may overlap the edges of the frame.
Behind and around it: a symbolic scene of the story, e.g. a large waving national flag, a map of the country
highlighted in glowing gold with red arrows or a red "no" symbol, the country's landmark government building,
oil barrels, stacks of US dollars, a burning refinery or oil field with black smoke, warships, fighter jets,
missiles, a red falling stock chart.
Colours: rich and saturated, warm orange/gold glow against deep blue and black; smoke, sparks, lens glow.
Hyper-detailed, photorealistic, high contrast, like a movie poster.
The lower 40% stays darker and simpler (a city skyline at dusk, smoke, dark water) because big headline banners
are placed there.
