# سبک کاور «افق مسکن» (نسخه‌ی اولیه؛ قابل ویرایش)
Viral Persian real-estate news thumbnail. Bold, dramatic collage composition.
Main subject BIG in the upper half: Tehran apartment towers, construction cranes, a house with a price tag or a
"for rent" sign, keys, a family looking at a building; cinematic golden-hour light.
Symbols: rising or falling price arrows, stacks of rial banknotes, a calculator, a red or green chart, the Tehran
skyline with Milad Tower.
Colours: warm orange and terracotta against deep brown and black; high contrast, like a movie poster.
The lower 40% stays darker and simpler because big headline banners are placed there.
