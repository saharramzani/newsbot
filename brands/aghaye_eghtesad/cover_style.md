# سبک کاور «آقای اقتصاد» (قابل ویرایش)
Viral Persian economy-channel thumbnail. Bold, dramatic collage composition.
The main subject is BIG in the upper half: close-up, sharp, cinematic rim light.
Behind it: a symbolic scene of the money story, e.g. stacks of Iranian rial and US dollar banknotes, gold coins and
bars, a big stock-market chart with arrows, a currency exchange board, the Tehran Grand Bazaar, the Central Bank
building, shopping carts and price tags for inflation, apartment buildings for housing news.
Colours: the outlet's brand colours, deep navy blue (#002659) and magenta/crimson (#D81E4E), with white highlights;
glossy, high contrast, like a movie poster. Avoid green and gold as main colours.
The lower 40% stays darker and simpler because big headline banners are placed there.
