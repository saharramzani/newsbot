# سبک کاور «آقای اقتصاد» (نسخه‌ی اولیه؛ قابل ویرایش)
Viral Persian economy-channel thumbnail. Bold, dramatic collage composition.
The main subject is BIG in the upper half: close-up, sharp, cinematic rim light.
Behind it: a symbolic scene of the money story, e.g. stacks of Iranian rial and US dollar banknotes, gold coins and
gold bars, a big green or red stock-market chart with arrows, a currency exchange board, the Tehran Grand Bazaar,
the Central Bank building, shopping carts and price tags for inflation, apartment buildings for housing news.
Colours: deep green and gold against black; glossy, high contrast, like a movie poster.
The lower 40% stays darker and simpler because big headline banners are placed there.
