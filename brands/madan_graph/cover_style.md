# سبک کاور «معدن گراف» (نسخه‌ی اولیه؛ قابل ویرایش)
Viral Persian mining-industry news thumbnail. Bold, dramatic, industrial collage composition.
Main subject BIG in the upper half: a giant open-pit mine, haul trucks, a steel mill with molten metal sparks,
copper cathodes, gold bars or iron ore pellets; cinematic rim light, dust and sparks.
Symbols: rising or falling commodity charts, a world map with trade arrows, stacked metal ingots, a commodity
exchange board.
Colours: copper and molten orange against charcoal grey and black; metallic, high contrast, like a movie poster.
The lower 40% stays darker and simpler because big headline banners are placed there.
